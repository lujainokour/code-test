    How your face or fingerprint is protected

    • Face ID, Windows Hello and Touch ID check you on your own device. CodeMentor AI never receives or stores images of your face, fingerprints or any biometric data.
    • Your device creates a private key that never leaves it. Our server keeps only the matching public key and checks a fresh, single-use signature each time you sign in.
    • Passkeys only work on this website's address, so a fake look-alike site can't use them.
    • Your password always remains as a backup. If you lose a device, sign in with your password and remove its passkey here.
